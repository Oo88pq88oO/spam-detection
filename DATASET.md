## Dataset

This project uses the **Enron-Spam Dataset**, containing 33,716 email messages:
- 17,171 spam emails
- 16,545 legitimate (ham) emails

The dataset was originally collected by V. Metsis, I. Androutsopoulos, and G. Paliouras.

**Original source:** [AUEB – Enron-Spam Dataset](http://www2.aueb.gr/users/ion/data/enron-spam/)

**CSV version:** [MWiechmann/enron_spam_data](https://github.com/MWiechmann/enron_spam_data)

The CSV version is a preprocessed version of the original dataset, provided by M. Wiechmann.

**Original research paper:**

Metsis, V., Androutsopoulos, I., & Paliouras, G. (2006). *Spam Filtering with Naive Bayes – Which Naive Bayes?* Proceedings of the 3rd Conference on Email and Anti-Spam (CEAS 2006).
